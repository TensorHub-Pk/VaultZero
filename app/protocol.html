Your Blueprint for Absolute Privacy

A transparent deep-dive into the Zero-Knowledge engine. Learn how to operate every button, control, and security layer with total confidence.

What it Does

VaultZero is a zero-knowledge encryption engine. It transforms your sensitive data into cryptographically secure noise using your own Master PIN as the root of trust. Unlike cloud services, VaultZero never sees your data, never stores your keys, and cannot recover your password.

Post-Quantum Ready

Hybrid key exchange (X25519 + ML-KEM) ensures security against future quantum threats.

Offline Autonomy

The entire application works without an internet connection once loaded.

How to Use

Operating VaultZero follows a strict "Lock & Release" cycle to ensure maximum memory safety.

1. Initialize

Set your Master PIN. This generates your local identity keys and unlocks the dashboard.

2. Execute

Perform your encryption or decryption. Use the 'Pulse' toggle to sync data if needed.

3. Wipe/Lock

Once done, lock the vault. Use 'Panic Wipe' in emergencies to shred all session keys.

When to Use

VaultZero is designed for scenarios where absolute privacy is the only acceptable standard.

Professional Ops

Handling corporate secrets, API keys, or legal documents on untrusted networks.

Secure Comms

Sending sensitive payloads to others via public channels like email or messaging apps.

Digital Archivism

Storing long-term encrypted backups that must remain secure for decades.

Interface & Operations

A complete breakdown of every button and control within the VaultZero ecosystem.

The Security Gateway

Master PIN Input

What it does: The root of your encryption. It is used as the salt for the Argon2id key derivation function.

How to use: Enter a 4-12 digit PIN. Warning: There is no "Forgot PIN" option. If you lose this, your data is gone forever.

Unlock Vault

What it does: Verifies the PIN against your local identity hash and initializes the dashboard.

How to use: Click after entering your PIN. The screen will transition to your active vault.

Password Manager

Add New Credential (+)

What it does: Opens the encryption overlay for a new site, username, and password entry.

How to use: Click the "+" icon, fill in the details, and hit "Save Securely". The data is instantly AES-256 encrypted.

Copy / View

What it does: Decrypts the specific entry into your clipboard or on-screen view.

How to use: Click the eye or clipboard icon. The plaintext is purged from memory after a 30-second timeout.

Secret Vault (Files & Notes)

Encrypt File/Note

What it does: Packages your data into a SHA-256 checked encryption envelope.

How to use: Drag a file or type a note. Set an optional expiration time. Click "Seal Secret".

Self-Destruct Timer

What it does: Adds an expiration metadata tag to the encrypted payload.

How to use: Select a duration (1h, 24h, etc.). After this time, the vault will refuse to decrypt the file.

Secure Share & PQC Identity

Generate Identity

What it does: Creates your Ed25519 signing keys and ML-KEM (Kyber) encapsulation keys.

How to use: One-click setup. Your "Public Identity" can now be shared with others to receive encrypted messages.

Hybrid-PQC Encrypt

What it does: Encrypts a message using a blend of classical and quantum-resistant algorithms.

How to use: Paste the recipient's Public ID, type your message, and hit "Generate Secure Link".

Steganographic Shield

Hide in Image

What it does: Uses 3-bit threshold encoding to conceal encrypted bits inside a PNG's pixels.

How to use: Upload a "Carrier" image and your secret data. Click "Embed & Download". The result looks like a normal image.

Extract from Image

What it does: Scans an image for the "VZSG" magic header and reconstructs the encrypted payload.

How to use: Upload the modified PNG. If a secret is found, you will be prompted for the decryption PIN.

Advanced System Controls

Titanium Pulse (Toggle)

What it does: Enables real-time synchronization across your linked devices via anonymous relays.

How to use: Switch to 'ON'. Your vault will now automatically sync changes to other tabs and devices.

Isolation Mode

What it does: Instantly severs all network APIs (fetch, WebSocket, etc.) for a totally air-gapped session.

How to use: Click to "Seal Environment". Note: This will disable Pulse Sync until you reload the app.

Panic Wipe

What it does: The ultimate safety switch. It shreds all local keys, clears IndexedDB, and terminates the session.

How to use: Click in emergencies. WARNING: This action is irreversible and deletes all local data.

Release Integrity & GPG
