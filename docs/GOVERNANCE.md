## 🏛️ Governance Philosophy
The VaultZero project is committed to open, transparent, and distributed governance. This model is designed to support our **OpenSSF Best Practices Gold Badge** requirements, ensuring a sustainable "bus factor" and shared responsibility for the project's security.

## 👥 Roles & Responsibilities

### 1. Contributors
Anyone who submits a contribution (code, documentation, bug reports). Contributors are the lifeblood of VaultZero.
- **Requirement**: Adherence to the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- **Requirement**: Following the [Contributing Guidelines](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

### 2. Maintainers
Individuals with broad write access responsible for day-to-day operations and strategic direction.
- **Two-Person Rule**: No maintainer may merge their own code without independent review.
- **MANDATORY 2FA**: Strict enforcement of 2FA (TOTP or Hardware Keys) for all write access.
- **Conflict of Interest**: Maintainers must disclose any affiliations that could impact project neutrality.

## ⚖️ Decision-Making Process
1. **Public Discussion**: Major architectural changes must be discussed in public community forums.
2. **Consensus**: Maintainers aim for a 100% consensus on security-critical decisions.
3. **Voting**: If consensus fails, a simple majority among maintainers decides.

## 🚌 Access Continuity (Bus Factor Protection)
To ensure project longevity, we implement a strict Access Continuity Plan:
- **Shared Administrative Rights**: Ownership of the repository and deployment infrastructure is shared between at least two unassociated maintainers.
- **Break-Glass Access**: Critical infrastructure credentials are stored in secure, offline environments for emergency recovery.
- **Contributor Diversity**: We actively recruit maintainers from different backgrounds and organizations to prevent centralized control.
